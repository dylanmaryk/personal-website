    No folding iPhone?

    Then there is no hinge to read, and the app says so.

    The reading is labelled Entirely made up, and you get a slider and a row of presets to set the angle yourself — exactly the controls you have been using on this page. Everything else works the same. It is not the real thing. It is close enough.

    “Your iPhone folds. Now you can know exactly how far, to the degree, at all times. There is no second feature. We are as surprised as you are.”

    What it does not do

    Nothing, thoroughly.

    It reads the hinge, prints the number, says something unkind about it, and stops. There is no settings screen, because there is nothing to set.
