Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Ajar is an iPhone app that displays the current angle of a folding iPhone's hinge, along with a short line of commentary about it.

Ajar does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, makes no network requests, contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and includes no third-party software development kits. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere or seen by anyone but you.

Information the app does not collect

Ajar does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact information
  • Account credentials, because the app has no accounts and no sign-in
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, calendars, health data, or any other data from your device
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or diagnostic data

The app requests no permissions and presents no permission prompts, because it needs access to none of the protected data or sensors that require them.

Information stored on your device

Ajar stores one piece of information on your iPhone: the angle you last set by hand using the slider or preset buttons, saved as a single number so the app reopens at the same angle. It is stored locally using the system's standard app preferences and never leaves your device.

The hinge angle itself is read from your device's hardware while the app is open. It is displayed to you and is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Deleting the app removes the stored angle from your device.

Apple Intelligence and on-device generation

On iPhones where Apple Intelligence is available and enabled, the line of commentary below the angle is generated using Apple's on-device model, through Apple's Foundation Models framework.

This processing happens entirely on your iPhone. The request sent to the model contains only the current hinge angle, a description of the app, and the previous line of commentary so it is not repeated. It contains nothing about you, your device or your activity. No part of it is sent to Apple, to the developer, or to any other server.

Where Apple Intelligence is unavailable, turned off, or set to a language the model does not support, the app uses its own built-in lines instead. The rest of the app is unchanged.

Sharing

If you choose to share an angle, the app creates an image and a short line of text and hands them to the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you start it, and you choose where it goes.

Once you send a shared card to another app or service — Messages, Mail, a social network, or anything else — that app or service handles it under its own privacy policy, not this one. The developer of Ajar receives no copy of anything you share and has no record that you shared it.

Children's privacy

Ajar is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to review, correct or request the deletion of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised “Last updated” date. If a future version of the app ever collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
